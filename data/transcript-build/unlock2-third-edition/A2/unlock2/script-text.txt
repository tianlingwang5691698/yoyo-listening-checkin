Narrator: Hi everyone. Today's topic is 'somewhere to live'. I want to talk about somewhere that is not the coldest and not the hottest place – but the farthest away. Tristan da Cunha is an island located in the South Atlantic Ocean. It's inhabited – that means people live there. But Tristan da Cunha is really far from anywhere else. It really is in the middle of nowhere. The nearest place is South Africa, 1,750 miles away, that's 2,816 kilometres. South America is 2,088 miles, or 3,360 kilometres, to the east.
And how many people live on this island? It's not crowded at all. Currently, the population is less than 300. There are around 80 families, and there are only seven last names. They would certainly recognize a stranger right away. They speak English, but it's a strange form of English, maybe because these people live so far away from other English speakers. People have lived on the island since about 1810. But the volcano on the island is ancient, almost 3 million years old.
So, would you like to live on this island? There are many advantages. First of all, life is very simple. Everyone knows each other, and there's no crime on the island. So, I believe it's completely safe to leave your car or house door unlocked. No one will take anything. Since it isn't crowded, you don't have any traffic problems. It's also very clean. You can drink water from the rivers. I think this all sounds great – how often can we do this in other countries? Finally, I think there are many beautiful things to see – mountains, a volcano – and it's a good place if you like watching birds. In many ways, Tristan da Cunha is a beautiful place, and I think that people feel really free there.
I'd also like to talk about disadvantages. It's a very rural place. The only jobs there are related to farming and fishing. It's difficult to leave the island – only a few boats go there every year. And the trip from South Africa takes six days. I believe this makes a lot of people lonely. Also, it isn't a really modern place. Television only arrived in 2001, and there is very little internet access. And you shouldn't expect things like movie theatres, coffee shops or shopping malls. There aren't any.
So, in summary, I think that Tristan da Cunha is a really interesting place to visit, if you have lots of time and money. However, I don't think it would be easy to live there. In my opinion, there are more disadvantages than advantages. If you want a really simple, easy life, and if you want to feel free, then that's good. But if you want to meet a lot of people, and have a modern lifestyle, then I think it would be a really bad experience. But what do you think? Would you like to live somewhere simple and free?

Narrator: There are many festivals in Japan. I want to talk about one of the biggest celebrations. It happens every year. Everyone can see and enjoy it. It takes place in every town and city in the country, from the hot south to the cold north.
This festival is called hanami. In Japanese, hana means flower, and mi means to watch. This is a celebration of watching flowers – or to be more exact, viewing the little pink flowers that appear on cherry trees all over the country. This is the cherry blossom festival, and it happens every year after the end of winter.
There are millions of cherry trees in Japan, and for a few short weeks, whole cities turn pink. It's a really beautiful sight. In the south, the pink flowers start to appear in the early spring. By the beginning of May, this slowly spreads to the north. Japanese people watch the daily TV news to find out when the hanami will come to their area.
There are many well-known places where you can see thousands of trees together. For instance, Ueno Park in Tokyo is perhaps the most famous place, and people have many parties here in the spring. During the day, many people take photos of the cherry flowers. At night, beautiful paper lanterns decorate the trees. Everyone has a good time being with other people and enjoying the beauty of nature.
This is a happy festival. It's traditional for people to go to the festival with their family or the people they work with. They sit under the trees and eat food or sing songs. They eat special food, such as dango – a colourful sweet made from rice. Other examples of dishes they enjoy are fried meat and vegetables or onigiri – rice balls with tasty fillings.
This tradition is spreading to other countries, too. You can celebrate hanami with Japanese communities in the US or Australia. Even countries like Finland are starting to have their own flower-watching festivals.
But I think this is also a festival with some sadness. After a short time, the cherry flowers die and fall off the trees. Then the trees grow new leaves. Japanese people know that life is short. Everything changes and gets old, so we should celebrate beautiful things while we can.

It is because more technology is available to children than ever before. These things can be important learning tools, but they can also create a lot of problems.

Also, some experts believe that technology might have a negative effect. Some children can feel isolated and have poor communication skills if they spend too much time on computers. Children should play with other children. They can learn a lot by playing – about nature and the world. Sitting in front of a computer for hours stops a child from doing this and doesn’t help them to communicate with other people face to face. Some researchers think children should wait until they are older to use technology.
So, on the one hand, most people agree that children should use technology. Children who can’t use a computer, mobile devices or the internet will have a lot of problems when they start going to school or when they become adults. Also, technology can teach children a lot. There are a lot of educational games and apps that children can enjoy and the internet has a lot of information to learn from, too. On the other hand, if really young children spend too much time with a computer or mobile device, then that is a problem. A computer or a tablet is not a babysitter. Children need to learn about the real world and how to be with other people. That’s why parents and teachers need to make sure that children only spend a short time on a computer or mobile device every day.
Professor: Thank you very much, Shadia. Does anyone have any questions?
